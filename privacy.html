Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Studia · Last updated 15 October 2026

Summary

Studia does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your data stays with you

  • Your courses, timetable, assignments, plans and grades are stored only on your device.
  • If you turn on iCloud sync, the data is stored in your own iCloud Drive, under your Apple Account. The developer cannot access it.
  • You can export or delete all of your data at any time in Settings → Data & backup, or by deleting the app.

Network access

When you paste a timetable or learning-platform calendar link (.ics), Studia downloads that calendar file directly from the server you provided (for example your university). Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. Links you open, such as course profiles, open in your browser.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is used.

Children

Studia does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

简要说明

Studia 不收集任何个人数据。没有账号、没有统计分析、没有广告，也不做任何追踪。

数据只在你自己手里

  • 课程、课表、作业、计划和成绩只保存在你的设备上。
  • 如果打开 iCloud 同步，数据存在你自己的 iCloud 云盘里，开发者无法访问。
  • 随时可以在「设置 → 数据与备份」导出或清除全部数据，删除 App 也会一并删除。

联网

当你粘贴课表或学习平台的日历链接（.ics）时，Studia 只会从你提供的服务器（比如学校）直接下载这个日历文件，不会发给开发者或任何第三方。

通知

所有提醒都在你的设备上本地安排，不经过任何推送服务器。

变更

如有更新，会发布在本页面。
